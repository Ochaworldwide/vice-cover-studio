**Vice Panels** is a GTA-poster-style cover art maker that runs entirely in the browser. Drop in a photo, pick a multi-panel layout with angled white dividers, grade it like a Vice City sunset, add bold poster lettering, and export a share-ready PNG.

- **Multi-panel layouts** — Hero + Stack, Vice Strips, Grid Collage and Diagonal Slash, each panel clipping its photo with cover-fit.
- **Any photo, every panel** — upload or drag-and-drop your own photos (they never leave your machine), or start from the built-in samples. Click a panel to select it, swap its photo, drag to reposition, and zoom with a slider.
- **Four colour grades** — Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Golden Hour and Midnight Chrome, painted with canvas gradients and filters.
- **Poster lettering** — an editable main caption and subtitle in bold condensed capitals with a dark outline and glow, in Vice Pink, Chrome Gold or Neon Ice.
- **One-click export** — PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080, the common post sizes.

Everything is original: the art, lettering and layout are drawn fresh with Canvas 2D and system font stacks. No borrowed logos, artwork or fonts, no libraries, and no network requests — the app works fully offline from its static files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

then open http://localhost:8080
